---

**Legend**
- $P$ = Principal / Present Value
- $FV$ = Future Value
- $PMT$ = Payment
- $r$ = Interest Rate (per period)
- $n$ = Number of periods
- $t$ = Time
- $g$ = Growth rate
- $D$ = Dividend
- $D_1$ = Next period dividend
- $C$ = Coupon payment
- $F$ = Face value
- $CF$ = Cash flow
- $\text{CapEx}$ = Capital Expenditure
- $\text{D\&A}$ = Depreciation & Amortization
- $\Delta\text{WC}$ = Change in Working Capital
